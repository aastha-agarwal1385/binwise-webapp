# ♻️ BinWise

### AI-Assisted Smart Waste Reporting & Collection System

BinWise is a prototype for a smart waste management platform designed to make
waste reporting, segregation, collection, and community participation more
organized and accessible.

The platform explores how AI-assisted waste classification, citizen reporting,
NGO coordination, cleanliness analytics, and reward-based engagement can be
combined into a single digital solution.

---

## 🌱 Problem Statement

Improper waste segregation and inefficient waste collection can lead to
environmental pollution and make it difficult for communities and local
authorities to monitor waste-related issues effectively.

BinWise aims to provide a simple digital interface through which users can
report waste, identify its category, and connect waste-related information
with collection and community-level initiatives.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

BinWise brings different parts of the waste-management process together:

- 📸 Upload waste images for category identification
- ♻️ Classify waste into categories such as dry, wet, and plastic
- 📍 Report waste-related issues
- 🤝 Support coordination with NGOs and collection initiatives
- 📊 Monitor community/city-level cleanliness information
- 🌱 Display environmental impact indicators
- 🏆 Encourage participation through rewards and rankings
- 💡 Provide eco-friendly tips and facts

The current project is a functional prototype that demonstrates the proposed
user experience and workflow.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗑️ Smart Waste Reporting
Users can report waste-related issues through a simple digital interface.

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Waste Classification
The concept allows users to upload an image of waste and classify it into
categories such as:

- Dry waste
- Wet waste
- Plastic waste

### ♻️ Reusable & Donatable Items
Users can also share information about reusable or donatable items so that
they can potentially reach NGOs or other collection initiatives.

### 📊 Cleanliness Dashboard
A dashboard concept provides community/city-level indicators such as:

- Waste classification
- Cleanliness score
- Environmental impact
- CO₂ reduction indicators

### 🏆 Rewards & Rankings
A reward-based system is designed to encourage users to actively participate
in responsible waste management.

### 🌿 Eco Tips
The interface includes environmental facts and tips to encourage
sustainable habits.
